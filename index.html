About Me

I am a third-year PhD student in Computer Information Systems at Georgia State University, where I am fortunate to be advised by Yu-Kai Lin.

My research focuses on the intersection of public policy and Information Systems (IS), with a specific emphasis on the governance and societal implications of AI and digital innovation.

I'm always open to collaborating and discussing new ideas.
